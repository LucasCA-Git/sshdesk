# Code of Conduct

SSHDesk adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and welcoming, give and accept constructive feedback gracefully, focus on what is best for the community, and do not harass anyone — in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

Unacceptable behaviour can be reported privately to the maintainer through the contact options on [GitHub](https://github.com/LucasCA-Git). Reports will be handled confidentially, and maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions, or ban participants, who do not follow this code.
